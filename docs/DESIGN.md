# BuxbrewCharge — experience design

**Author:** Babuni

The addon adds theatrical rhythm to everyday fighting. Its interaction grammar is: **player presses chosen button → occasionally a built-in emote plays → the ability still proceeds**. Randomness comes from *which* emote fires, not from a probabilistic on/off chance; the current gate is time-based only.

## User-facing controls

There is no options frame. Discovery and configuration take place in chat with `/bbc watch`, `/bbc slot <n>` and `/bbc info`. Status messages use a blue `BBC:` prefix. The chosen slot is shared across the account, which is convenient but can need resetting when different characters arrange their bars differently.

## Tone

The pool combines overt battle presence (CHARGE, ATTACKTARGET, ROAR), pride and delight (CHEER, FLEX), and playful interruptions (TRAIN, LAUGH, CHUCKLE, BORED, WHISTLE). The mixture gives a warrior-style action a less predictable personality. There are no bundled sounds, graphics or generated texts.

## Accessibility / usability to verify

Ensure status messages remain readable in chat; the player can disable watch-mode noise; emotes do not suppress the actual skill; character-language/localization does not break built-in tokens. These are quality checks, not claimed test results.
